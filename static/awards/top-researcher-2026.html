01. How to read this data

  • Scopus-based citation indicators compiled by John P. A. Ioannidis and colleagues at Stanford University.
  • Single-year impact: based on citations received during 2025 by previously published papers.
  • Career-long impact: a separate dataset assessing citations accumulated over a research career. This file alone does not establish inclusion in that category.
  • (ns) means self-citations are excluded. The corresponding field without this suffix includes self-citations.
  • These are citation-based indicators and do not capture every dimension of research quality.

Percentages are approximate: subfield rank divided by the number of researchers in that subfield. They are not overall percentiles.
